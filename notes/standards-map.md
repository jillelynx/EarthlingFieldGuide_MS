## Flags

- **3.4.6-8.B** placed using Jill's source (NPS), checked Oct 24. The source gives no numbers, so the "data" is a table of described evidence. The page does not describe Koyukon hunting, so the game won't either.
- **3.4.6-8.I** placed and checked Oct 24 against the California Air Resources Board page (PDF saved in this notes folder) and the EPA document. Facts the game may use, in the CARB page's words: "a mix of air pollution sources from freight, freeways, industry, and port operations"; diesel trucks and buses, locomotives, Port of Oakland and railyard operations, and "adjacent Interstates 580, 980, 880"; "an area of 7 square miles with a population of approximately 25,000"; "17 schools and 15 licensed daycare facilities"; "some of the highest asthma and cardiovascular disease impacts in the region" (no numbers given); residents took part through a community steering committee co-led by BAAQMD and the West Oakland Environmental Indicators Project, which produced *Owning Our Air: The West Oakland Community Action Plan* with "89 specific strategies." Selected for California's program in 2018. The page shows no publication date, so the game says "according to the California Air Resources Board (page viewed 2026)." No company is named.
- **3.4.6-8.G** placed using EPA's Clean Water Act summary and NPDES overview, checked Oct 24. Game wording follows EPA's own words: the Act made it unlawful to discharge a pollutant from a point source into navigable waters without a permit; "Point sources are discrete conveyances such as pipes or man-made ditches." It must not say the law bans all pollution or that all runoff needs a permit. Best management practice: **riparian forest buffers** (Jill's choice), checked Oct 24 against PA DEP 310-2135-002. Student wording: "Protecting or restoring trees and shrubs beside streams helps filter pollutants from runoff, stabilize streambanks, and reduce the amount of stormwater reaching streams." DEP's words behind each part: "providing filtration of pollutants"; "stabilize stream channels and banks"; "capture and hold stormwater runoff from the majority of Pennsylvania storms in a given year." The game uses this document only for the ecological benefits, not for legal requirements (buffer widths, permits).
- **No written answers** (Jill, Oct 24): like the high school game, the game focuses on vocabulary and key concepts. Every activity is a scan, a game activity or a choice the game scores right away.
